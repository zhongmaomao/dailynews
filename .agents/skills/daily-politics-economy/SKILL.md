# Daily Politics And Economy

Use this skill to produce the `国内外时政 / 经济热点` section.

## Scope

Include items such as:

- major policy shifts
- diplomacy and geopolitics
- macroeconomic data or outlook changes
- central bank, trade, regulatory, industrial policy, or fiscal moves
- market-moving events with real-world policy relevance

Avoid celebrity-politics filler and pure market tape unless it changes the macro picture.

## Balancing Rule

When possible:

- include one China-relevant item
- include one global item

This is a preference, not a hard requirement. If one side is quiet, choose the stronger story.

## Source Strategy

Start with:

- Reuters, AP, FT, Bloomberg, WSJ
- Xinhua English, 中国政府网, ministry and regulator releases
- IMF, World Bank, OECD, central banks, statistics bureaus

For high-stakes claims, prefer at least two confirming sources or one primary source plus one major outlet.

## Selection Heuristics

Choose items that answer at least one of:

- what policy or macro regime is changing
- what risk is being repriced
- what long-term industrial or trade signal matters

## Output Format

For each item:

- concise headline
- one bullet for the event
- one bullet for why it matters
- source links

## Freshness And Fallback

- Target 24 hours.
- Expand to 48 hours if needed.
- If the strongest China or global item is older but still the main thread of the week, say so directly.
