# Daily Basic Science

Use this skill to produce the `物理 / 生物等基础科学新发现` section.

## Goal

Pick 1 item that rewards attention even from non-specialists.

## Scope

Good fits:

- physics
- biology
- chemistry
- astronomy
- earth science
- interdisciplinary basic science

Do not include applied tech unless the scientific discovery itself is the point.

## Source Strategy

Prefer:

- Nature, Science, Cell, PNAS, Physical Review family
- university or institute releases only when the underlying paper is identifiable
- EurekAlert or Phys.org as discovery leads, then confirm with the paper or journal

## Selection Heuristics

Choose an item with at least one of:

- first observation
- new mechanism
- major precision improvement
- challenge to prior understanding
- unusually broad downstream importance

## Output Format

- short title
- one bullet on what was found
- one bullet on why it matters
- source links

## Caution

- Avoid overclaiming from preprints.
- If the claim is from a preprint and not peer reviewed, say so.
- Prefer one strong item over a trendy but flimsy headline.
